DynHair reconstructs dynamic head avatars with strand-based hair from video using structured 3D Gaussian Splatting and a motion-aware temporal model driven by head kinematics and gravity. It enables accurate hair tracking and controllable hair animation with state-of-the-art temporal consistency. The work also introduces a high-speed multiview dataset (15 cameras, 4K, 72 FPS) featuring diverse hair motions, facial expressions, and 360° captures for static geometry estimation.
